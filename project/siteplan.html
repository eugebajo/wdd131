Team Zana–Website Planning Document

Site Name

Team Zana A personal training and fitness brand that focuses on weekly workout routines, health tips, and personalized training plans.

Why this name? This is the real brand name used by the trainer on Instagram (@teamzana.py), making it authentic and recognizable.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to promote the personal training services of Team Zana, provide weekly workout content, offer health and nutrition tips, and allow clients to contact the trainer for bookings and personalized plans.

Scenarios

  • How can I contact Team Zana to book a personal training session?
  • Where can I watch the latest “Workout of the Week” video?
  • What kind of exercises are recommended for beginners?

Color Scheme

Main Colors:

  • #FFD700 (Gold) Used for headings, highlights, and call-to-action buttons.
  • #1E1E1E (Dark Gray) Used for text and navigation background.
  • #FFFFFF (White) Used for body background and card backgrounds.

Typography

  • Montserrat Used for headings and navigation menus.
  • Roboto Used for body text and paragraphs.
